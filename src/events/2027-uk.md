Circular Steel UK brings together the people who actually deliver steel reuse: clients, engineers, contractors, fabricators, stockholders, demolition teams, researchers and policymakers. One day in London, built around real projects and the problems that come with them.

Steel reuse is one of the largest and least exploited levers for cutting embodied carbon in construction. The barriers are rarely technical - they are commercial, contractual, and about whether the right material and the right project meet at the right moment.

Since 2022, Circular Steel UK has grown into the UK's focal point for structural steel reuse. The 2026 event brought over 125 people from across the supply chain together for a day of case studies, roundtables and honest conversation about what is and is not working. [Watch the 2026 sessions](/uk/2026/).

## Who it's for

Circular Steel UK is built for people with a decision to make:

- **Clients and developers** deciding whether reuse is viable on a live scheme
- **Structural engineers** specifying reclaimed sections and carrying the design risk
- **Main contractors and project managers** pricing and programming reuse
- **Demolition and enabling contractors** recovering material with a market in mind
- **Stockholders, fabricators and steel producers** making the supply side work
- **Cost consultants and lawyers** turning it into something that stacks up commercially
- **Researchers, institutions and policymakers** shaping standards and regulation

## What happens on the day

**Keynotes and panels.** Short, direct sessions on the state of steel reuse, the economics, and where policy is heading. No sales pitches.

**Private roundtables.** Small, chaired discussions on a single question, with a mix of clients, designers and supply chain in each room.

**Drop-in clinics.** Bring a live problem to a specialist and talk it through in a small group. No preparation needed.

**Case study boards.** Real projects presented in a consistent format across the day, curated rather than sold.

**Breakfast, lunch and networking drinks.** The reason many people come, and we plan them accordingly.

## A few questions people ask

**Will sessions be recorded?**
Yes. Sessions are filmed and made available free afterwards, as they were in 2026.

**Is it technical?**
Some of it. Sessions are pitched at people who need to make a decision rather than at specialists only, and the drop-in clinics exist precisely so you can ask the question you'd rather not ask in a big room.

**Do sponsors choose the speakers?**
No. Sponsorship buys visibility and, at the higher tiers, a role in the programme. It does not buy any say over content or speakers.
