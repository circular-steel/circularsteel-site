Circular Steel returned for a second year, with a larger and more diverse group of speakers and attendees. It was a massive success, with amazing feedback from the 140 attendees and speakers.

The event was filmed, with complete footage from the panels plus over 20 short clips available on a dedicated [YouTube shorts playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6ooiEl5hrA9shOkz_SZeF4NDbBwdTExQ). Photography was by Amphitype, filming by EMS Events.

Thank you again to the 2023 Partners, speakers and attendees for making it happen.

## Panels

**Testing, Specifications & Data** — Chair: Penny Gowler (Elliott Wood); Dr Michael Sansom (BCSA); Laura Batty (HTS); Jonathan Davis (William Hare Ltd); David Brown (SCI); Peter Muszynski (Zurich)

**Inventory, Availability & Tools** — Chair: Martin Quinn (GPE); Christian van Maaren (Excess Materials Exchange); Roy Fishwick (Cleveland Steel & Tubes); Diego Padilla-Philipps (WSP); Tom Howarth (Elliott Wood, now EMR); Andrea Charlson (Useful Projects)

**Low Carbon Steel Production** — Chair: Will Arnold (IStructE); Matthew Teague (Tata Steel); Walter Swann (Arcelor Mittal); Roger Morton (EMR); Sameen Khan (Climate Group/SteelZero); Dave Knight (CARES)

**Purchasing Reusable Steel** — Chair: Dan Morrissey (Skanska); Bill Firth (EMR); Roy Fishwick (Cleveland Steel & Tubes); Adriano Amorese (Mishcon de Reya); Peter Kelly (ISG); Nigel Moss (Bourne Group)

**Duncan's Closing Speech** — Duncan Baker-Brown (Baker-Brown)
