**Elliott Wood, Platinum Sponsor** — hear from Gary Elliott, Louisa Treadwell and Andy Downey, who travelled to Miami Beach to share their knowledge and experience of steel reuse in the UK. Watch it below, under "More from the day".

## Miami Beach

CS USA 2025 was an invite-only, workshop-style event with 30 leaders from the US and UK built environment. Circular Steel partnered with WAF (World Architecture Festival) 2025 to make the event happen.
