Elliott Wood engineers "a better society". A certified B Corp, the practice offers structural engineering, sustainability, development infrastructure, transport and digital services.

## Why Circular Steel

Around 90% of Elliott Wood's 2023 workload was adaptation or reuse. Two live proof-of-scale projects show what's possible: 180 Piccadilly, involving roughly 480 tonnes of reused steel and believed to be the largest UK reuse project to date, and 20 Giltspur Street, where a floor was jacked up to reuse steel in place.
