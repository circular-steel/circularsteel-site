Heyne Tillett Steel (HTS) is an employee-owned structural, civil and geotechnical practice, established in 2007 and Certified B Corp, with 180+ staff across London and Manchester. Recent projects include Holbein Gardens for Grosvenor, which won Structural and ICE awards and spawned the HTS Stockmatcher tool, and 76 Southbank, the Grade II listed Denys Lasdun IBM building, for Stanhope. HTS also runs its own self-funded research initiative, HTS+.

## Why Circular Steel

A founding member of the Engineers Reuse Collective, with research partnerships including Innovate UK and Grosvenor.
