Acquired in 2025 by Sev.en Global Investments, 7 Steel UK is the UK's leading low-carbon, circular steel producer from scrap, running an Electric Arc Furnace facility in Cardiff with capacity of up to 1.2 million tonnes a year.

## Why Circular Steel

The circular economy sits at the core of 7 Steel UK's operating philosophy, spanning recycling, new products and circular-economy solutions.
