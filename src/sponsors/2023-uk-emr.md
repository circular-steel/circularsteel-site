EMR is a global sustainable materials company with operations across the UK, USA, Germany and the Netherlands, recycling more than 10 million tonnes of metal and plastics every year and saving over 15 million tonnes of CO2 compared with virgin ore.

## Why Circular Steel

EMR has a net-zero target of 2040. Its Reusable Steel activities operate at 47kg CO2e per tonne, verified by an Environmental Product Declaration.
