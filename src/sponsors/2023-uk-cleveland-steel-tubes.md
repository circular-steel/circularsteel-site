Now in its 50th year trading in reused steel, supplying tubular and H/I sections for construction and civil engineering projects, including steel used in stadia and sporting venues.

## Why Circular Steel

A supporter of Circular Steel since the inaugural event. Cleveland Steel & Tubes has worked with SCI and BCSA on the P427 Reuse Protocol, Annex J and the Model Purchase Specification, and is currently working on two SCI innovation projects looking at the reuse of older steel.
